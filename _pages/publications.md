    <strong>Current Status:</strong> I am currently submitting manuscripts and conducting doctoral research at <strong>IIT Goa</strong>. My work addresses both the theoretical and computational challenges associated with Stochastic Partial Differential Equations (SPDEs).

  <h2 class="mt-4" style="font-weight: 600; border-bottom: 1px solid #eee; padding-bottom: 10px;">Primary Research Directions</h2>
  <p class="mt-3">
    My doctoral research investigates advanced mathematical concepts within the realm of parabolic SPDEs. Key areas of investigation include:
  </p>
  
  <ul class="list-group list-group-flush mb-4">
    <li class="list-group-item bg-transparent">
      <strong>Theoretical SPDEs:</strong> Investigating the well-posedness and pathwise regularity for multidimensional semilinear SPDEs with locally Lipschitz coefficients and rough initial data using a semigroup theoretic approach.
    </li>
    <li class="list-group-item bg-transparent">
      <strong>Computational SPDEs:</strong> Developing robust numerical algorithms, specifically focusing on optimal and strong error estimates of Finite Element Methods (FEM) for semilinear SPDEs with both additive and multiplicative noise.
    </li>
  </ul>
